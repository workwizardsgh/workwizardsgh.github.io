QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is WorkWizards?
WorkWizards is a student-focused academic and tech service in Ghana. We help students finish their projects, reports and technical work properly and on time. You order, pay, track and receive everything through our Telegram bot, @WorkWizards_TechBot.
Do I need Telegram to order?
Yes. Telegram is free on Android, iPhone and computer. Install it, search @WorkWizards_TechBot and tap Start. Asking about your work costs nothing.
How do I place an order?
  1. Open the bot and tap Start.
  2. Pick the service you need and answer a few quick questions.
  3. Send your files: guidelines, drafts, data or your supervisor's comments.
  4. Add a promo code if you have one.
  5. An admin reviews your work and sends your price.
How much does it cost?
There's no fixed price list, because every job is different in size, difficulty and deadline. An admin reviews your work and sends you a price in the bot. You don't pay anything until you accept it.
How do I pay?
  • By Mobile Money.
  • Pay 50% to start the work and the balance before your final files are sent.
  • You get a receipt for every payment.
Can I see my work before I pay the balance?
Yes. We send you a protected preview in the chat so you can check the work before paying the rest. The final files come after the full payment.
How long will my work take?
You tell us your deadline when you order, and the admin confirms it with your price. Short deadlines are possible, depending on the work.
Can I follow the progress of my order?
Yes. The bot updates you as your work moves forward, so you always know where it stands without having to ask.
What if my supervisor asks for corrections?
Send the corrections through the bot and we'll handle them. This takes away the endless back-and-forth with your supervisor.
Is my information private?
Yes. Your work and details are only seen by the WorkWizards team handling your order, and your files are deleted after 30 days.
I have a promo code. How do I use it?
Enter it when the bot asks for a promo code while you're ordering. The discount is applied to your price after the admin reviews your work. Each code can be used once per student.
Which schools do you work with?
Students from universities, technical universities, nursing and midwifery colleges and colleges of education across Ghana. Tell us your school when you order, so we follow its format.
How do I contact you?
Message us in the bot, @WorkWizards_TechBot, or call 020 020 7537 or 059 470 6801.
